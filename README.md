# Kurason Guahan Wallet

## Problem
Residents of Guam are losing money to pyramid schemes like SFCVIBES. 
There's no unified platform to share fraud alerts and regulatory info.

## Solution
A community wallet + fraud alert platform where Guam residents can:
- Control their own crypto assets
- Share scam warnings
- Access regulatory contact info
- Report frauds to authorities

## Features
- Multi-chain support (Ethereum, Base, Tron)
- Fraud alert community feed
- Regulatory contact directory
- Educational resources
- Exchange, buy, swap, sell and transfer to bank account, bank card and Paypal.
